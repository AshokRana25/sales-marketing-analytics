    Metric definitions & interpretation
    Paid ROAS
    Net sales revenue assigned to paid-search and paid-social campaigns / ad spend. Observed attribution is not proof of incremental lift.
    MER
    Total net revenue / ad spend. Available in the report API; distinct from paid ROAS because it includes email and organic sales.
    CTR / CPC
    Total clicks / total impressions × 100; total spend / total clicks. Ratios use summed inputs, not averages of row ratios.
    Cost per ad conversion
    Ad spend / advertising-platform conversions. This is not customer acquisition cost; unique customers are not available.
    Orders / AOV
    Count of accepted distinct order IDs, including refunded orders; net revenue / that count. Zero denominators show an em dash, not zero efficiency.
    Attribution and coverage
    One campaign label per sales order, with exact date/channel/campaign matching. No cross-device or multi-touch model. Missing source days are flagged, and currency is USD only.
